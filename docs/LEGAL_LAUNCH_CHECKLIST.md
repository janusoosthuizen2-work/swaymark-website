Do not invent an address or publish a home address without deliberate consent and consideration of privacy. POPIA section 18 requires notice of the responsible party's name and address; ECTA section 43 includes physical address and service address disclosures when the business offers goods or services by electronic transaction. Have a South African attorney assess which disclosures apply to the present enquiry-only website.

A preliminary public web search found other unrelated businesses using SWAYMARK or SWAYMARK variants outside South Africa. This is NOT a registrability or infringement determination.

On incorporation, update all legal pages, contracts, invoices, provider accounts, schema identity and registration details. Confirm with an adviser how any pre-incorporation contracts are handled.
- [ ] **SARS and invoices.** Declare business profits through the correct sole-trader personal income tax process and use suitable business records. Check the latest VAT rules and thresholds before registering or adding VAT to an invoice. Do not invent a VAT number.
- [ ] **Information Officer and PAIA.** Determine and complete applicable registration of the Information Officer with the Information Regulator, and PAIA manual or other requirements. Establish a practical process for privacy requests, secure access, data retention and incident response.
- [ ] **Third-party data processing.** Evaluate Cloudflare Pages, Google email, FormSubmit and any cross-border data-processing arrangements. Check contracts, retention, breach notification and security commitments. Seek a lawful operator arrangement under POPIA as appropriate.
- [ ] **Project agreement template.** Before taking paid projects, create a written proposal/agreement covering legal parties, services, scope, dependencies, acceptance, revision limits, change orders, timeline, price and currency, taxes, deposits and payment, cancellation/refunds, consumer rights, intellectual property, website/domain ownership, client data and access, confidentiality, support and disputes.
