A little introduction

I’m Janus.
I work where design, marketing and the technical stuff meet.

My day-to-day work has given me a practical view of what businesses actually need online. I’ve worked on website management and SEO, Google visibility, content writing, campaign material, performance improvements and the reporting that connects the dots.

A large part of that experience comes from supporting the online presence of a Perth-based service business while working remotely from South Africa. It’s taught me to pay attention to the details that customers may never see — but absolutely feel.

I’m building this independent studio around that approach: strong design, honest communication, sensible strategy and the willingness to keep improving things long after launch.
