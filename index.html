06 / Good to know

Questions?
Good.

Here are a few things people usually want to know before getting started.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. We’ll discuss what your business needs, the pages and features involved, and the best approach for your budget and goals. I can also help improve an existing website.

Do you only build websites, or can you manage them too?

Both. I can help with updates, content changes, SEO, speed improvements and ongoing care. We’ll agree on what’s included so you know exactly what to expect.

Can you help with marketing and social media?

Yes. I can support campaign ideas, marketing creative, content planning, social posts, search visibility and measurement. The mix depends on your brand and what you want to achieve.

Will my website automatically rank first on Google?

No one can honestly promise that. I focus on useful content, sound SEO fundamentals, technical quality and ongoing improvements rather than guaranteed rankings.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Yes. My work already includes supporting a business in Australia, and website and marketing projects can often be handled remotely.
