Privacy / Your information

Clear about your privacy.

How SWAYMARK handles your information when you contact us, and what choices and rights you have.

Your information is treated with care.We request only what is reasonably needed to discuss your enquiry. Sending an enquiry does not add you to a marketing mailing list.

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Responsible party: Janus Oosthuizen, an individual operating in South Africa under the trading name SWAYMARK (not currently an incorporated company). For privacy questions or requests, email janusoosthuizen2@gmail.com or call 070 434 7735.

1. Information collected and your choice

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email address, the type of service you need and a short project message. Those fields are required so we can understand and respond to the enquiry. Your business name and preferred start date are optional. You choose whether to contact us; if required details are not provided, we may not be able to respond. Please do not include passwords, payment card information, identity documents or sensitive personal information.

2. Why information is used

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, discuss suitable services, prepare quotations and, if you later engage us, manage the agreed work and necessary business records. Information may also be processed where reasonably necessary for security or to comply with applicable law. We do not treat an enquiry as permission to send unrelated direct-marketing emails.

3. How the contact form works

When you click Send enquiry, the details you enter are submitted over HTTPS to FormSubmit, an external service which filters spam and forwards the message to the SWAYMARK email inbox. The site does not operate its own enquiry database. A hidden spam-prevention field is used and FormSubmit may require a Google reCAPTCHA challenge. If a reCAPTCHA appears, Google may process technical information according to its privacy policy and terms. You may contact us directly by email or telephone instead of using the form.

4. Hosting, email and service providers

The primary website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; an older version remains accessible through GitHub Pages. Our email communications use an email provider. Hosting, email, spam-prevention and form-delivery providers may process relevant technical data, such as IP addresses, request logs and the information necessary to deliver a message or protect their services. We do not currently operate advertising pixels, site analytics scripts or marketing cookies on SWAYMARK pages; a separate spam-check page may use its provider's own cookies or similar technologies.

5. Disclosure and international processing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with FormSubmit, our email provider and infrastructure or professional service providers where needed to deal with your enquiry or operate our services. Some providers or systems may process or store information outside South Africa. Where applicable, the cross-border processing requirements of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and other relevant laws must be met.

6. Retention and safeguards

FormSubmit's published help information says submissions are retained for 30 days. Copies delivered to SWAYMARK by email, and any records created if you become a client, may be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for their intended purpose, legitimate business needs or legal obligations; after that they should be deleted or de-identified where appropriate. We aim to apply reasonable security safeguards and limit access to enquiry information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion where permitted, or object to certain processing. You can also ask how your information has been used. Contact janusoosthuizen2@gmail.com and provide enough detail for us to identify your request safely. Some requests may be subject to legal exceptions or verification of your identity.

8. Complaints and further information

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa), including at POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, where the Regulator's processes apply.

9. External links and changes

Links to third-party websites are subject to their own practices and notices. We may update this notice when our services, legal status, providers or privacy practices change. Any material changes should be reflected on this page with a new update date.
