Privacy / Your information

Clear about your privacy.

How this website handles contact information and what you should know when getting in touch.

Your privacy matters.We only ask for information needed to respond to enquiries. Email us with any questions about how we handle your details.

Effective date: 15 November 2026. SWAYMARK is an independent digital-services business operated by Janus Oosthuizen. To discuss your information or make a privacy request, email janusoosthuizen2@gmail.com.

1. Information you provide

If you enquire, you may choose to provide your name, business name, email address, project needs and other details in your message. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card data or sensitive personal information.

2. How enquiries work

When you send the contact form, your name, email address, optional business name, selected service and timing, and project message are submitted over HTTPS to FormSubmit, an external form-processing service. FormSubmit processes your submission to filter spam and deliver it to SWAYMARK by email. The form does not send a message through your own email application, and the website itself does not operate a separate enquiry database. You can also email or call us directly instead of using the form.

FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days and uses anti-spam screening, including Google reCAPTCHA where a challenge is required. Completing a challenge may involve processing by Google under its privacy policy and terms. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information or sensitive personal information.

3. Why personal information is used

Information received through an enquiry is used to respond to your request, discuss potential services, provide quotes and, if we work together, administer that relationship. We do not treat an enquiry as consent to receive unrelated marketing emails.

4. Website hosting and technical data

The main website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with an older GitHub Pages address remaining available. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for delivery, security and abuse prevention. We do not currently run advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts or marketing cookies on our website. The external FormSubmit spam check may involve its own cookies or related technical data, subject to that provider’s privacy practices. If additional tracking or providers are introduced, this notice will be updated and applicable consent requirements reviewed.

5. Sharing, storage and retention

We do not sell personal information. Enquiries may be shared with FormSubmit and with email, hosting and security providers to receive and respond to your message. FormSubmit's stated archive retention is 30 days; email copies and business records may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain necessary records, fulfil contractual obligations or comply with applicable law, then deleted or de-identified as appropriate. Providers may process information outside your country; applicable cross-border privacy obligations still apply.

6. Security and your rights

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect the personal information that reaches our inbox, recognising that no online service is completely risk-free. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. You can make a privacy request by emailing janusoosthuizen2@gmail.com. Where applicable, you may also contact the relevant data protection authority if you have concerns.

7. External sites

Links to external services take you to third-party websites with their own privacy notices. We do not control their content or practices.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when features or providers change. For privacy enquiries, email janusoosthuizen2@gmail.com or visit the contact page.
