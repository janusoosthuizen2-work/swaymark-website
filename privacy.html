Privacy / Your information

Clear about your privacy.

How this website handles contact information and what you should know when getting in touch.

Publication checklist — importantThis is a tailored starting policy for a portfolio and enquiry website, not a legal sign-off. Before publishing, confirm the responsible party’s full business identity and public contact email, the actual hosting arrangements and any third-party tools or analytics you enable.

Effective date: 13 November 2026. This notice describes the current website template for Swaymark, operated by Janus Oosthuizen, an independent digital-services professional based in South Africa.

1. Information you provide

If you enquire, you may choose to provide your name, business name, email address, project needs and other details in your message. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card data or sensitive personal information.

2. How enquiries work

The current website uses an email-draft form. Your browser prepares an email for you to review and send using your own email application. This website does not submit form data to its own server or save a copy of your enquiry. If you decide to send an email, your email provider and the recipient’s email provider process that communication according to their respective policies.

3. Why personal information is used

Information received through an enquiry is used to respond to your request, discuss potential services, provide quotes and, if we work together, administer that relationship. We do not treat an enquiry as consent to receive unrelated marketing emails.

4. Website hosting and technical data

The site is intended to use a static hosting provider. Hosts may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for delivery, security and abuse prevention. Check the selected provider’s privacy documentation before launch. This template does not include analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies. If those are later added, this notice must be updated and consent requirements reviewed.

5. Sharing, storage and retention

We do not sell personal information. Enquiry emails may be processed by email and service providers involved in delivering and securing our services. Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, contract obligations or applicable legal requirements, then securely deleted or de-identified as appropriate. International processing may occur when using global service providers, subject to the applicable legal requirements.

6. Security and your rights

Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards should be used to protect personal information. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. Please make requests through the published contact method once the site goes live. You can also contact South Africa’s Information Regulator if you have concerns.

7. External sites

Links to Instagram or other services take you to third-party websites with their own privacy notices. We do not control their content or practices.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when features or providers change. For privacy enquiries, use the business email displayed on the contact page after it is configured for public use.
